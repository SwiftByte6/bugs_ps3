# User Accessibility Preferences and Adaptations

The system adapts its presentation and interaction modalities based on user-configured accessibility settings without inferring or diagnosing disabilities.

## Preference Categories
- **screen_reader**: Optimizes layout for screen reader flow, enables verbose ARIA announcements, and ensures full semantics.
- **voice_navigation**: Activates microphone listening for speech navigation and form interaction commands.
- **keyboard_navigation**: Enhances focus indicators, shortcut overlays, and provides sequential key access.
- **captions**: Displays real-time synchronized text captions for audio prompts, interview simulations, and alerts.
- **text_first**: Guarantees that all content, alerts, and instructions are presented visually in plain text before audio or media.
- **text_to_speech**: Enables audio read-aloud buttons for job descriptions, form questions, and system responses.
- **speech_to_text**: Enables dictation inputs for textual answers and notes.
- **large_text**: Scales base typography to 125%-150% without truncating UI elements or requiring horizontal scrolling.
- **high_contrast**: Applies WCAG AAA compliant color contrast (7:1 ratio minimum) with crisp foreground/background boundaries.
- **reduced_motion**: Disables decorative animations, transitions, and carousel auto-advancements.
- **dyslexia_mode**: Employs dyslexia-friendly fonts, generous line-height (1.6x+), and distinct word spacing.
- **simplified_language**: Converts complex corporate jargon into clear, plain-language bullet points.
- **eye_control**: Enables alternative hands-free interaction using camera-based eye landmarks and blinks.
- **preferred_language**: Supports localization in English, Hindi, and Marathi while preserving technical terminology.
